## Why this exists

OpenRMM is released under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0. The Owner
also offers the same software to customers under separate commercial terms.

Doing both requires that one party hold the rights to the whole codebase. If a
contribution arrives under the AGPL alone, the Owner cannot include it in a
commercially licensed build without that contributor's individual permission —
and a project that has to chase down every past contributor to change anything
is a project that can no longer change.

This agreement solves that **without taking your work away from you**. You keep
the copyright in what you write. You grant the Owner a broad licence to use it,
including under licences other than the AGPL.

## How to sign

Signing is per person, once, and covers every Contribution you make afterwards.

1. Open a pull request adding one line to [`CONTRIBUTORS.md`](CONTRIBUTORS.md):

   ```
   - Full Name <email@example.com> (GitHub: @handle) — signed <YYYY-MM-DD>
   ```

2. In that pull request's description, include:

   > I have read the OpenRMM Contributor License Agreement and I agree to it.
   > I am signing on my own behalf.

   If you are signing for a company, replace the second line with the company's
   legal name and confirm you are authorised to bind it.

That pull request, made from your own GitHub account, is your signature. Ask
first if anything above is unclear or does not work for you — it is easier to
discuss before you write the code than after.
