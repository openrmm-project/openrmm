## Forks

Forks are welcome; that is what the AGPL is for.

- Give the fork its own name and logo.
- Take the OpenRMM ones out of the interface, the installers and the images.
- Keep the licence, the copyright notices and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES` as they
  are.
- Saying "based on OpenRMM" is fine.
